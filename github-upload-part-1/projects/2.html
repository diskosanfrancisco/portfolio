O2 Grow

The contract of your life

With O₂ Grow, data grows as life does — and we brought this to life in our campaign, where a phone booth became a metaphor for the mobile contract itself. Step inside, and you see how life expands — and your tariff grows with it.

KV

The campaign got its own name and icon, with key visuals inspired by scenes from the film.

PR Stunt

We made sure everyone could feel this evolution: all users were upgraded at once, receiving more data for free. The moment everyone got the message created a big PR buzz and pleasantly surprised users.

Photo

We held an extra photoshoot during filming to provide fresh visuals for social media and online formats.

Offer

For those who aren’t yet customers but want to enjoy all the benefits, we launched a special offer and promoted it across all channels.
