Re-wine

SimpleWine is a Moscow-based wine store that sells wines selected by experts. This company also has

a sommelier school and is considered an expert

in wine issues. They approached us in the post-covid period to understand how to attract attention to the store in conditions of restrictions on alcohol ad communication and to fix in people's minds that thanks to the expert selection of wines, everyone can find something to their liking in the SimpleWine store.

‍

We found a great solution, which drew attention to the company and helped people in a difficult period.
