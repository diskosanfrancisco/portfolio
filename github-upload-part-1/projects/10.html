Birdwatching Moscow

Moscow

Birdwatching Moscow is a group of passionate

and knowledgeable scientists who share a common

goal of promoting nature conservation. They offer bird tours in Moscow and around the world for those who are interested in learning more about these beautiful creatures. By joining their tours, individuals can gain

a deeper understanding of birds and their behavior while also enjoying like-minded company.

‍

Birdwatching Moscow aims to inspire people to pursue their childhood dreams of becoming naturalists, cultivating a fascinating hobby, and building new connections.

The main trick

in this identity

are the eyes

The shape of the eyes change depending on the place where the birds are being watched (forest, mountains, etc. and transforms depending on the task at hand. One thing remains unchanged: the eyes always look in the direction of the bird.
