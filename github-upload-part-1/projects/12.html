Road safety

Signs

Drivers often drive faster than a traffic sign shows: radars and cameras do not notice a slight excess. This is dangerous: the location and meaning of the signs were calculated by engineers who studied the features of stretch of the road. The hero of the video is a shaman who also likes to interpret signs in two ways. But even he knows that the road sign is always unequivocal.
