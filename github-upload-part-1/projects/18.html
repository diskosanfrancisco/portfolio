Azbuka Vkusa

Christmas style and KV

Azbuka vkusa is a Russian private chain of food supermarkets in Moscow and St. Petersburg. They also include bakeries, catering, and even their own farm. New Year is the most long-awaited holiday. We're not just waiting for the day of the holidays. We wait for the magic that comes with them. This is the feeling that the posters are dedicated to. They show everything we can't wait to do during the holidays.

Concept

Our New Year campaign's design captures the joy and thrill of the holiday season through visual cues related to time. Clock hands, Roman numerals, and dial divisions were transformed into festive symbols like snowflakes and frost patterns on a window. Even the numbers got a decorative embellishment, adding to the overall cool design. The clock hand shaped like a Christmas tree is a perfect touch, making it a unique and inspiring design.

Design

The New Year's campaign went beyond just posters and extended to the cafe within the supermarket, where branded goods and products were also available for purchase in the new design.
