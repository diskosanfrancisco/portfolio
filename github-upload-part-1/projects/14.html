Launch Audi Q3

Seriously?

The launch of the New Audi Q3 in the Russian market has prompted the need to explain to a younger audience that it's not just a family crossover. The refreshed Audi Q3 has undergone significant changes. We devised the slogan "Serious like an Audi. Exciting like a Q3" to convey its strict but creative nature and use it as the foundation for a bright 360-degree advertising campaign.
