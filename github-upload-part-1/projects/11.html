Campaign · Selected work

Bahroma

Orient ice cream

In the Middle East, there are lots of definitions that don’t have equivalents in other languages. Feelings they describe can not be translated, but you can feel them or try them.

سعادة sa'āda

TV

The campaign is based on words that have no counterparts in other languages. The TV spot illustrates the word sa'āda

A feeling of complete satisfaction and joy.

The pleasure associated with sunrise.

KV

For the qv series, we used three other words and abstractly visualized the feelings they meant.

راحة rāḥa

The feeling of pure serenity refers to rest in Eastern culture. Rahat loukoum means rest for your mouth.

سعادة sa'āda

A feeling of complete satisfaction and joy.

The pleasure associated with sunrise.

نشوة

naŠwa

Euphoria, emotional arousal. «Ethereal love» in classical Arabic literature.

Special project

Bahroma.kz

In a special project, you could select images, videos and music and get an idea of how you were feeling at the moment. As a result, the site called out an oriental word and suggested an ice cream flavour depending on the user's mood.
